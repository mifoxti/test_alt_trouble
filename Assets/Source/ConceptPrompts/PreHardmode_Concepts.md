# Prompt notes: pre-Hardmode concept sheets

These generated images are proposal sheets only. Keep their current PNG files under `Assets/Previews`; do not load them as gameplay textures.

## Cursed Grove

Terraria-scale side-view pixel-art environment with Pilgrim Tree (thin reaching branches, sparse burgundy leaves), Hollow Elder (broad hunched trunk and warm-lit hollow), and Weeping Tree (drooping foliage); walkable dark soil, twisting roots, a grave marker, small broken roadside shrine, and a few warm Candle Moth points of light. The mood is abandoned sacred woodland slowly reclaimed by nature, not a Corruption reskin. Crisp pixel clusters, restrained charcoal/brown/moss/burgundy/amber palette.

## Dormant Eternal Altar

Three clearly separated compact crafting-station concepts at consistent scale: carved wood with cloth and gold; saintbone arch over a stone base; stone relief and sacred emblem. All include three candles, bone details where appropriate, muted burgundy cloth, weak warm rune light, and Terraria-readable silhouette. No final sprite frames.

## First Cursed Grove enemies

Three side-view silhouettes on a neutral background: Withered Root, a broken black-wood stump with long root arms and red eyes; Candle Moth, cream wings, dark body, wax growth and small amber glow; Hollow Pilgrim, hunched hooded traveler with void face, scarf, staff and hanging relics. Show one readable pose and small detail callouts, not animation sheets.
