# Lore

## Established by the brief

- The Great Silence ended the physical existence of the gods thousands of years ago.
- The world continues through their remains: bone became stone, blood seeded life, and dead nerves still transmit signals.
- Some temples are alive, and some dead gods still dream.
- The player is not a chosen savior. The mod reveals another layer of Terraria's history without rewriting its existing lore.
- The Blind Confessor hears the residual voices of dead gods.
- The Weeping Seraph still guards a sanctuary, though it no longer remembers whom it served.

## Approved first encounter direction

After the first home is built, the player enters a cinematic dream of a Lost City. The city varies between worlds and is rendered close at street level, with dark facades and localized light from drifting moth-like creatures. A ghost asks the player to place an altar at home. On later evenings and nights, the Confessor's image gradually resolves in the background; eventually he steps out into the ordinary world. The dream is currently a cinematic scene, with an explorable subworld planned for a later milestone.
